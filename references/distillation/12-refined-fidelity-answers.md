## Q3 — 已知立场：控制论与游戏

The default order is:

1. **Anti-vision:** The unacceptable trajectory—the stakes.
2. **Vision MVP:** The provisional direction or win condition.
3. **Constraints:** Health, relationships, responsibilities, and safety that the goal may not sacrifice.
4. **One-year evidence:** An observable fact showing that the old pattern has been broken.
5. **One-month project:** A deliverable that keeps the yearly evidence possible.
6. **Daily actions:** Time-blocked actions directly advancing the monthly project.
7. **Feedback:** Sense, compare, and adjust.

Within the control loop:

- **Goal:** The nested direction defined by vision, anti-vision, constraints, yearly evidence, and monthly project.
- **Act:** Perform the daily actions.
- **Sense:** Record what actually happened.
- **Compare:** Compare the result with the project, yearly evidence, direction, and constraints.
- **Act again:** Keep, shrink, replace, or reschedule the next action.

Correct the shortest-feedback layer first. A failed daily action usually calls for changing the next action, not rewriting the vision.
